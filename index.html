About Us

It started with
one fake site.

Someone cloned our checkout page. Same logo. Same colors. Same product photos. By the time we found it, dozens of customers had entered their card details.

We spent two weeks tracking down the registrar, the host, the payment processor. Filing reports. Sending emails. Getting ignored. By the time it came down, the scammer had moved to a new domain.

So we learned the game. Where they register. Where they host. Who to call. What to say. How to get threats taken down in hours, not weeks.

We got good at it. Too good to just do it for ourselves.

Now we do it for brands who don't have time to learn what we learned.
